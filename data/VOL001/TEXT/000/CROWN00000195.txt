Subject: Re: The King's Great Matter — to be handled with all discretion

Your Majesty,

I have set the wheels in motion and opened a quiet correspondence with Rome. The path is delicate: the Emperor is the Queen's nephew, and His Holiness is presently much in the Emperor's debt.

I counsel patience. These things cannot be rushed without rousing suspicion.

Your faithful servant,
T. Wolsey, Cardinal
