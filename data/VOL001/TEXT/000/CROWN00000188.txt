From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Cardinal Thomas Wolsey <thomas.wolsey@canterbury.church.tudor>
Cc: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Tuesday, 17 May 1527 08:30 AM
Subject: The King's Great Matter — to be handled with all discretion

My Lord Cardinal,

You know the disquiet that has long sat upon my conscience touching my marriage to the Queen. The matter can wait no longer.

I charge you to obtain from His Holiness a clean and lawful annulment, and to do so quietly. Spare no effort, and spare me no excuses.

I need not remind you what hangs upon a male heir.

By the King's own hand,
Henry R.
