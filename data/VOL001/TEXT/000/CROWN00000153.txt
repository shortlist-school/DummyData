INTERNAL - PRIVY COUNCIL
Privy Council Notes — 05 December 1526
Present: a quorum of the Council. Chaired by Queen Anne Boleyn.
Matters discussed:
  - the French embassy: noted and referred for action.
  - the muster of able men: noted and referred for action.
  - the Scottish border: noted and referred for action.
No other business. The Council rose at the eleventh hour.
