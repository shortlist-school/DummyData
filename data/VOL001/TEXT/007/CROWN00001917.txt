Subject: The muster of able men in hampshire (quarterly)

My good lord,

I must raise a concern about the muster of able men in Hampshire; the costs have run ahead of the estimate and I would not commit further without your knowledge.

Your faithful servant,
Archbishop Thomas Cranmer
