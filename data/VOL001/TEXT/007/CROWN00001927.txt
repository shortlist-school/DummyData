TUDOR TIMES WEEKLY — week of 19 May 1546
Your trusted source for crown, court and countryside.

THIS WEEK IN HISTORY:
  * Henry VIII reigned from 1509 to 1547 — nearly thirty-eight years.
  * The tune 'Greensleeves' is popularly — if doubtfully — attributed to the king himself.
  * Catherine Parr was the first woman to publish a book in English under her own name in England.

COURT WHISPERS: New French fashions arrive at Greenwich — ruffs are said to be growing.

WEATHER: Grey, with a chance of beheadings. (Editorial humour; no offence intended.)
