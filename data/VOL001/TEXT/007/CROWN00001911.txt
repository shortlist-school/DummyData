From: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>, Ambassador Eustace Chapuys <e.chapuys@aragon.gov.tudor>
Sent: Monday, 11 March 1546 09:05 AM
Subject: Note: the muster of able men in Sussex

Greetings,

Arrangements for the muster of able men in Sussex are as follows: carts at dawn, the household to follow, and the heavy plate to go by water.

Ever at your command,
Archbishop Thomas Cranmer
