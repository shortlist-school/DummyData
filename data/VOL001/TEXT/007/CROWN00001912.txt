Subject: Concerning the King's physicians' reports

Worthy councillor,

I am not wholly easy about the King's physicians' reports, and would value a quiet word before we commit anything to writing.

That motion was dead from the neck up, frankly.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Archbishop Thomas Cranmer
