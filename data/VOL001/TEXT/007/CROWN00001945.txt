From: Bishop Stephen Gardiner <stephen.gardiner@canterbury.church.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Tuesday, 09 July 1546 07:00 PM
Subject: Concerning certain books in the Queen's chamber

Your Majesty,

It is my duty, however unwelcome, to note that certain reformist books have been seen in the Queen's apartments, and that Her Majesty debates points of religion more freely than is perhaps seemly.

I raise it only that Your Majesty may judge.

Your servant,
S. Gardiner
