Subject: Concerning the New Year's gifts

To my esteemed colleague,

The matter of the New Year's gifts grows pressing and cannot be left to lie much longer.

Keep your head about you in tomorrow's council.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Sir Edward Seymour
