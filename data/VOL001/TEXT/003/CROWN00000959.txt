Sent: Sunday, 19 January 1536 02:45 PM
Subject: the coronation arrangements — for your consideration

Good morrow,

The council sat this morning touching the coronation arrangements. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

With all due reverence,
Sir Henry Norris
