From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>, Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley <thomas.audley@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Saturday, 07 September 1535 02:05 PM
Subject: the lead and bells of Glastonbury Abbey — action required

My good lord,

The council sat this morning touching the lead and bells of Glastonbury Abbey. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

In haste,
King Henry VIII

--
Sent from my writing-desk. Pray excuse any brevity or blots.
