From: Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 30 January 1536 04:00 PM
Subject: I am grieved

My lord,

You will have heard. I am as grieved as you, and more. I beg you not to turn from me for a misfortune that God alone governs.

Do not let the whisperers at court make of my sorrow a weapon against me.

Your unhappy wife,
Anne
