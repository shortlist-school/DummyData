From: Charles Brandon, Duke of Suffolk <charles.brandon@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>, Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley <thomas.audley@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 14 April 1535 09:05 PM
Subject: the Queen's household expenses — for your consideration

To my esteemed colleague,

The council sat this morning touching the Queen's household expenses. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

In haste,
Charles Brandon, Duke of Suffolk
