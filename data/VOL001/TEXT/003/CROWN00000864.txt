From: Sir Henry Norris <henry.norris@crown.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Monday, 29 April 1535 02:45 PM
Subject: the peers' summons to the Tower — for your consideration

Greetings,

Further to the peers' summons to the Tower: the arrangements are in hand and I will report again within the week.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

Written in great expedition,
Sir Henry Norris

--
Sent from my writing-desk. Pray excuse any brevity or blots.
