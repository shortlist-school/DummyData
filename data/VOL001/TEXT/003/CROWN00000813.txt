Subject: the Queen's apartments at Greenwich (further to our last)

Most gracious madam,

The matter of the Queen's apartments at Greenwich grows pressing and cannot be left to lie much longer.

A weight off my shoulders — and, for once, nothing else.

Your faithful servant,
Sir Henry Norris
