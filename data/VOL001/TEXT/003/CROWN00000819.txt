From: Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Charles Brandon, Duke of Suffolk <charles.brandon@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 18 November 1534 11:00 AM
Subject: Concerning the Queen's household expenses

Good morrow,

Pray send me your mind on the Queen's household expenses before the next sitting; I would not act without it.

Yours in loyal service,
Queen Anne Boleyn

Sent from my writing-desk. Pray excuse any brevity or blots.
