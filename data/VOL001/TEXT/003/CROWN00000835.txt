Sent: Sunday, 20 January 1535 08:00 AM
Subject: Visitation of the religious houses — articles enclosed
Attachments: Visitation_Articles.docx

Sir Richard,

The great survey begins. I enclose the articles. Send out the commissioners and let them be thorough — the Crown's needs are pressing and the harvest of this work will be considerable.

Accurate returns, mind. I will not have the valuations padded nor pared.

Cromwell
