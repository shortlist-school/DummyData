Subject: Minutes touching the peers' summons to the Tower

Most gracious madam,

The council sat this morning touching the peers' summons to the Tower. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Sir Richard Rich
