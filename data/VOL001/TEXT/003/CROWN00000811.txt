Sent: Wednesday, 31 October 1534 05:25 PM
Subject: The repair of the great clock (quarterly)

My good lord,

Arrangements for the repair of the great clock are as follows: carts at dawn, the household to follow, and the heavy plate to go by water.

Yours in loyal service,
Lady Mary Tudor
