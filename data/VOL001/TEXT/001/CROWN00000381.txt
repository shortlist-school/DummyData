Sent: Saturday, 27 July 1529 02:15 PM
Subject: Query on the universities' verdicts

Right trusty and well-beloved,

I am not wholly easy about the universities' verdicts, and would value a quiet word before we commit anything to writing.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

Until the next sitting of the council,
Cardinal Thomas Wolsey
