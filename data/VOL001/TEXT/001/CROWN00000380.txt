From: Cardinal Thomas Wolsey <thomas.wolsey@canterbury.church.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Tuesday, 23 July 1529 04:30 PM
Subject: The legatine court at Blackfriars

Your Majesty,

I must report, with a heavy heart, that the cause has been revoked to Rome. The court will give no verdict here. I have failed to bring it to the close you desired, though not for want of labour.

In sorrow,
T. Wolsey
