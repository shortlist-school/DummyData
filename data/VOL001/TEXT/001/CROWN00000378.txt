Subject: Note on the Imperial ambassador's protests

To my esteemed colleague,

Further to the Imperial ambassador's protests: the arrangements are in hand and I will report again within the week.

A weight off my shoulders — and, for once, nothing else.

Your faithful servant,
Queen Catherine of Aragon
