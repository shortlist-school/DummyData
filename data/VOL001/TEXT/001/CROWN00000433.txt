Subject: The citation from Rome (in Latin)
Attachments: Citatio_Apostolica.pdf

Your Majesty, my lord Archbishop,

The long-awaited instrument has arrived from Rome. It is in Latin, as ever, and I attach it for the King's scholars to construe at leisure.

The short of it is this: Rome insists the cause be heard there, and not here. I need hardly say how Your Majesty will receive that news.

Your servant,
Thomas Cromwell
