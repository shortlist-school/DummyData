From: Sir Edward Seymour <edward.seymour@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Ambassador Eustace Chapuys <e.chapuys@aragon.gov.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Monday, 16 February 1542 08:15 PM
Subject: Note: the repair of the great clock

Good morrow,

Arrangements for the repair of the great clock are as follows: carts at dawn, the household to follow, and the heavy plate to go by water.

That motion was dead from the neck up, frankly.

Your faithful servant,
Sir Edward Seymour
