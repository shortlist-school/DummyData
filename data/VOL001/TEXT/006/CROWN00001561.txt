Subject: The wine shipment from gascony — for approval

Good morrow,

I write to request a decision on the wine shipment from Gascony before the quarter-day.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

Written in great expedition,
Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire
