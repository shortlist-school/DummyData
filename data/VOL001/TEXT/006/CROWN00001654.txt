From: Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley <thomas.audley@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Sir Edward Seymour <edward.seymour@crown.gov.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Tuesday, 18 May 1543 10:00 AM
Subject: Note: the progress to Hampton Court Palace

Right trusty and well-beloved,

I pray Your favour and approval for the progress to Hampton Court Palace; the matter waits only on your word.

Yours in loyal service,
Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley
