Your Majesty,

I enclose a plan for the children's learning. Edward thrives at his books, Elizabeth outpaces her tutors, and I have asked the Lady Mary to join us more often. A household at peace is worth a province.

I should be glad of Your Majesty's approval.

Your loving Catherine
