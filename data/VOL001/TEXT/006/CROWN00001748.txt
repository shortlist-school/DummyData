From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Wednesday, 19 April 1544 01:25 PM
Subject: The wine shipment from gascony

Good morrow,

I write to request a decision on the wine shipment from Gascony before the quarter-day.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
King Henry VIII
