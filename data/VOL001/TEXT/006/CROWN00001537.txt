To: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>
Cc: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>, Bishop Stephen Gardiner <stephen.gardiner@canterbury.church.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 06 November 1541 09:00 AM
Subject: Re: A matter I cannot bring myself to speak

Cranmer,

Examine it to the bottom, and quietly, before the court takes the scent. Question the lady of the bedchamber first; the go-between always knows the most.

Recover every letter. I will not be made a jest of in my own household.

Henry R.
