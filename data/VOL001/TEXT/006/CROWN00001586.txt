TUDOR TIMES WEEKLY — week of 19 May 1542
Your trusted source for crown, court and countryside.

THIS WEEK IN HISTORY:
  * The tune 'Greensleeves' is popularly — if doubtfully — attributed to the king himself.
  * Henry was a noted musician, linguist and jouster in his youth.
  * Anne Boleyn's daughter, Elizabeth, would reign for forty-four years.

COURT WHISPERS: Wagers are being laid on the date of the next royal progress.

WEATHER: Grey, with a chance of beheadings. (Editorial humour; no offence intended.)
