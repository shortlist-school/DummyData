Sent: Wednesday, 24 September 1541 06:25 AM
Subject: The royal stables accounts

Honoured sir,

I must raise a concern about the royal stables accounts; the costs have run ahead of the estimate and I would not commit further without your knowledge.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

Until the next sitting of the council,
Sir Richard Rich
