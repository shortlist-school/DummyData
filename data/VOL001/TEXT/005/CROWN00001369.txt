From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>, Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Saturday, 06 July 1540 08:00 AM
Subject: The Cleves marriage — a quiet remedy

My lords,

The marriage was never to my liking and, I am advised, never fully made. Find me the lawful means to undo it — quietly, and with all courtesy to the lady, who is blameless in this.

Henry R.
