From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Queen Catherine Howard <catherine.howard@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 01 August 1540 07:12 PM
Subject: Minutes touching the Queen's jewels and plate

Good morrow,

All is in order regarding the Queen's jewels and plate. There is nothing further that need trouble you for now.

Written in great expedition,
King Henry VIII

--
This dispatch is intended for the named recipient and may contain matters touching the Crown. If delivered in error, return it to the Master of the Posts and destroy all copies.
