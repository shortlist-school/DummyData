From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Culpeper <thomas.culpeper@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Saturday, 05 April 1541 06:10 PM
Subject: Note: the progress to Hampton Court Palace

Good morrow,

Touching the progress to Hampton Court Palace, I have engaged the usual tradesmen and set the dates. Details to follow under separate cover.

Written in great expedition,
King Henry VIII
