Subject: Re: A most advantageous match — portrait enclosed

Cromwell,

I have met the lady at Rochester. I will say only that the portrait was a great deal more agreeable than the introduction, and that Master Holbein has a diplomat's eye for flattery.

I am not well handled in this. We will speak privately, and soon.

Henry R.
