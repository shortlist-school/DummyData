Subject: The subsidy assessment for essex — for approval

Most gracious madam,

I must raise a concern about the subsidy assessment for Essex; the costs have run ahead of the estimate and I would not commit further without your knowledge.

Keep your head about you in tomorrow's council.

In haste,
Sir Edward Seymour
