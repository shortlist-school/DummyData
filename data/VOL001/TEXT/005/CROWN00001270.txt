From: Anne of Cleves <anne.cleves@kleve.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 14 September 1539 11:50 AM
Subject: The progress to hampton court palace (quarterly)

Worthy councillor,

I write to request a decision on the progress to Hampton Court Palace before the quarter-day.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Anne of Cleves
