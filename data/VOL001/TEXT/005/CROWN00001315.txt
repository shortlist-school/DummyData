From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 14 January 1540 07:30 AM
Subject: Query on the Cleves alliance

To my esteemed colleague,

The council sat this morning touching the Cleves alliance. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

Written in great expedition,
King Henry VIII

--
