To: Bishop Stephen Gardiner <stephen.gardiner@canterbury.church.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 15 October 1539 05:05 PM
Subject: Note on the pensions of the religious
Attachments: Notes_dissolution_nar-00133.docx

Worthy councillor,

The council sat this morning touching the pensions of the religious. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

Your faithful servant,
Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley
