From: Anne of Cleves <anne.cleves@kleve.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 11 July 1540 11:00 AM
Subject: With no hard feeling

Your Majesty,

I accept the judgement freely and gladly. I would far rather be Your Majesty's beloved sister than an unhappy wife. England suits me, and I find I am very fond of it.

Let us be friends. I hear the hunting at Richmond is excellent.

Your affectionate sister,
Anne of Cleves
