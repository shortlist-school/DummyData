INTERNAL - PRIVY COUNCIL
Privy Council Notes — 17 January 1541
Present: a quorum of the Council. Chaired by Queen Catherine Howard.
Matters discussed:
  - the new coinage: noted and referred for action.
  - the tiltyard schedule: noted and referred for action.
  - the Scottish border: noted and referred for action.
No other business. The Council rose at the eleventh hour.
