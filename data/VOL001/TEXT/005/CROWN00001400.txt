From: Francis Dereham <francis.dereham@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Queen Catherine Howard <catherine.howard@crown.gov.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 20 September 1540 02:45 PM
Subject: Minutes touching the Queen's jewels and plate

My good lord,

Pray send me your mind on the Queen's jewels and plate before the next sitting; I would not act without it.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

Written in great expedition,
Francis Dereham
