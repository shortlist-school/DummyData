TUDOR TIMES WEEKLY — week of 26 September 1539
Your trusted source for crown, court and countryside.

THIS WEEK IN HISTORY:
  * Henry was a noted musician, linguist and jouster in his youth.
  * Henry's children Mary I, Elizabeth I and Edward VI each took the throne in turn.
  * England's currency in this period reckoned in pounds, marks, shillings and pence.

COURT WHISPERS: New French fashions arrive at Greenwich — ruffs are said to be growing.

WEATHER: Grey, with a chance of beheadings. (Editorial humour; no offence intended.)
