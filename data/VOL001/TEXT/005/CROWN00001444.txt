From: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>
To: Bishop Stephen Gardiner <stephen.gardiner@canterbury.church.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 31 January 1541 08:15 PM
Subject: The embassy to france (quarterly)

Greetings,

I write to request a decision on the embassy to France before the quarter-day.

Written in great expedition,
Archbishop Thomas Cranmer
