From: Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>
To: Bishop Stephen Gardiner <stephen.gardiner@canterbury.church.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 25 August 1539 12:20 PM
Subject: Concerning the lady's reception at Greenwich

Greetings,

The matter of the lady's reception at Greenwich grows pressing and cannot be left to lie much longer.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

Your faithful servant,
Archbishop Thomas Cranmer
