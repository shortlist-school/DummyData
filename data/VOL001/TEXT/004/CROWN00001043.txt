From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley <thomas.audley@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 06 November 1536 08:50 AM
Subject: The kitchen accounts for the quarter

Honoured sir,

You have my leave for the kitchen accounts for the quarter. Keep the expense within reason.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

In haste,
King Henry VIII
