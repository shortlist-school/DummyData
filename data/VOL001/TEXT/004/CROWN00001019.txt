Subject: Query on the Court of Augmentations accounts

To my esteemed colleague,

I am not wholly easy about the Court of Augmentations accounts, and would value a quiet word before we commit anything to writing.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

Until the next sitting of the council,
Lord Chancellor Thomas Audley
