From: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Wednesday, 14 October 1536 07:30 AM
Subject: Trouble in the North

My lords,

There is a rising in Lincolnshire and Yorkshire, calling itself the Pilgrimage of Grace. They demand the abbeys be spared and Cromwell be dismissed — in that order, which I take as a compliment to the Master Secretary.

We will need a firm hand and fair words both.

In haste,
Ric. Rich
