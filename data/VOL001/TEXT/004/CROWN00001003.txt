From: Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Saturday, 16 May 1536 06:00 AM
Subject: From a faithful wife

Sir,

You have raised me from a gentlewoman to a marchioness, from a marchioness to a queen. There remains only to raise me, as you intend, to be a saint in heaven.

I ask only an open trial, and that my honour be not buried with me. Whatever becomes of me, our daughter is innocent of all.

From my cheerless lodging in the Tower,
Anne
