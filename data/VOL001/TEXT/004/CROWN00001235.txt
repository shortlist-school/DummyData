From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire <thomas.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>, Ambassador Eustace Chapuys <e.chapuys@aragon.gov.tudor>
Sent: Wednesday, 21 June 1539 08:40 AM
Subject: Note: the Garter ceremony arrangements

Worthy councillor,

I pray Your favour and approval for the Garter ceremony arrangements; the matter waits only on your word.

Let us not lose our heads over the seating plan.

Ever at your command,
King Henry VIII
