Subject: Concerning the survey of Walsingham Priory

Good morrow,

Further to the survey of Walsingham Priory: the arrangements are in hand and I will report again within the week.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Sir Richard Rich
