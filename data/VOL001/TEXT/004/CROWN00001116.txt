From: Thomas Culpeper <thomas.culpeper@crown.gov.tudor>
To: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 12 December 1537 11:10 AM
Subject: The wardrobe accounts — for approval

Good morrow,

I am uneasy about the wardrobe accounts and would welcome a quiet word at your convenience.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

Written in great expedition,
Thomas Culpeper

--
Sent from my writing-desk. Pray excuse any brevity or blots.
