From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 10 July 1536 10:15 AM
Subject: A word about the pensions of the religious

Honoured sir,

The council sat this morning touching the pensions of the religious. Opinions were divided, but the King's will carried the day.

Let's nip this one in the bud before it goes to the block.

Yours in loyal service,
King Henry VIII
