From: King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Charles Brandon, Duke of Suffolk <charles.brandon@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Monday, 18 May 1536 08:00 PM
Subject: The arrangements

Charles,

I have sent to Calais for the French swordsman, as a mercy. They tell me his aim is very good — which is more than can be said for half my council.

See that all is done with dignity, and quietly. Then we will speak no more of it.

Henry R.
