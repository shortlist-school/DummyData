From: Thomas Culpeper <thomas.culpeper@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>, King Henry VIII <henry.rex@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Sunday, 26 June 1538 04:05 PM
Subject: The victualling of the fleet — for approval

Honoured sir,

There is a difficulty with the victualling of the fleet that I think you should hear of before it grows.

Heads will roll if the venison is late a third time.

By the hand that serves the Crown,
Thomas Culpeper
