From: Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire <thomas.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Friday, 19 August 1538 07:00 PM
Subject: Note: the purveyance of venison

Good morrow,

May I have leave to proceed with the purveyance of venison? The cost is modest and the need real.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

Until the next sitting of the council,
Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire

--
Confidential — for the eyes of the Privy Council only.
