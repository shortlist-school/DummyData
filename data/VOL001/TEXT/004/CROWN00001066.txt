From: Thomas Culpeper <thomas.culpeper@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>, Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Saturday, 27 February 1537 08:05 PM
Subject: The wine shipment from gascony — for approval

Worthy councillor,

The plan for the wine shipment from Gascony is settled. Pray confirm you are content and I will give the word.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

Yours in loyal service,
Thomas Culpeper
