INTERNAL - PRIVY COUNCIL
Privy Council Notes — 07 September 1533 (rev. 2)
Present: a quorum of the Council. Chaired by Queen Anne Boleyn.
Matters discussed:
  - the victualling of the fleet: noted and referred for action.
  - the harvest levy: noted and referred for action.
  - the subsidy roll: noted and referred for action.
No other business. The Council rose at the eleventh hour.
