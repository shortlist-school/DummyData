Sent: Tuesday, 15 March 1532 08:40 PM
Subject: The royal stables accounts (quarterly)

Good morrow,

I must raise a concern about the royal stables accounts; the costs have run ahead of the estimate and I would not commit further without your knowledge.

He has been given the chop from the catering committee.

Yours in loyal service,
George Boleyn, Lord Rochford

--
Sent from my writing-desk. Pray excuse any brevity or blots.
