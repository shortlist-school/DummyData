TUDOR TIMES WEEKLY — week of 21 April 1533
Your trusted source for crown, court and countryside.

THIS WEEK IN HISTORY:
  * The Act of Supremacy of 1534 declared the king Supreme Head of the Church of England.
  * The tune 'Greensleeves' is popularly — if doubtfully — attributed to the king himself.
  * England's currency in this period reckoned in pounds, marks, shillings and pence.

COURT WHISPERS: The royal menagerie welcomes a most ill-tempered new leopard.

WEATHER: Grey, with a chance of beheadings. (Editorial humour; no offence intended.)
