Subject: A way through — privileged advice enclosed
Attachments: Opinion_on_Royal_Supremacy.pdf

Your Majesty,

Where Rome will not give, England may take. I enclose privileged advice setting out the lawful path: an Act restraining appeals, and recognition of Your Majesty as Supreme Head of the Church in England.

The Archbishop may then determine the Great Matter here at home. I commend the enclosed to Your Majesty's careful and private reading.

Your servant,
Thomas Cromwell
