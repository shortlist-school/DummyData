From: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>

Sent: Thursday, 22 June 1533 06:15 PM
Subject: Note: the progress to Richmond Palace

Greetings,

The plan for the progress to Richmond Palace is settled. Pray confirm you are content and I will give the word.

I shall stick my neck out and propose Thursday.

In haste,
Thomas Cromwell
