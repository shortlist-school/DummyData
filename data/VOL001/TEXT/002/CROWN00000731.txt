Sent: Thursday, 07 September 1533 07:30 PM
Subject: Our daughter
Attachments: Birth_Announcement_Princess_Elizabeth.pdf

My dear husband,

We have a daughter, strong and well, and I have named her Elizabeth for your lady mother. I know you hoped for a son. Sons will follow.

Come and see her. She has your temper already.

Your loving Anne
