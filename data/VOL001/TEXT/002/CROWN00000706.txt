Cc: Thomas Cromwell <thomas.cromwell@crown.gov.tudor>, Archbishop Thomas Cranmer <thomas.cranmer@canterbury.church.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 01 June 1533 12:00 PM
Subject: Proclamation — Queen Anne

My lords,

Let it be known throughout the realm that the Lady Anne is this day crowned Queen of England. You will accord Her Majesty all honour due to her estate.

Let us not lose our heads over the seating at the coronation feast — the Imperial ambassador is, regrettably, on the list.

Henry R.
