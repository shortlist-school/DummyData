From: Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire <thomas.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: George Boleyn, Lord Rochford <george.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>, Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Thursday, 21 January 1532 07:10 PM
Subject: Re: the wine shipment from Gascony

Honoured sir,

Arrangements for the wine shipment from Gascony are as follows: carts at dawn, the household to follow, and the heavy plate to go by water.

I trust this note finds you all in one piece.

Until the next sitting of the council,
Thomas Boleyn, Earl of Wiltshire
