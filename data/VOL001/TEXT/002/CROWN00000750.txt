From: Queen Anne Boleyn <anne.boleyn@crown.gov.tudor>
To: Sir Richard Rich <richard.rich@crown.gov.tudor>
Sent: Wednesday, 08 November 1533 06:40 PM
Subject: Re: the muster of able men in Sussex

Greetings,

I must raise a concern about the muster of able men in Sussex; the costs have run ahead of the estimate and I would not commit further without your knowledge.

Written in great expedition,
Queen Anne Boleyn
