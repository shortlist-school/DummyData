# Cast of Characters

Everyone who appears in the corpus, with their email address, role, the window
during which they are historically active (mail is only generated while they are
active), and a short historical note. All addresses use the reserved, fictional
`.tudor` top-level domain.

## A note on the affair web-mail accounts

The Howard Affair storyline deliberately uses **personal web-mail** addresses to
evade the household post — a realistic data-hiding behaviour and a deliberate
teaching point:

- Queen Catherine Howard — `kitty.h@ravenmail.tudor` (personal)
- Thomas Culpeper — `t.culpeper@ravenmail.tudor` (personal)

These messages were "recovered" and attributed to Queen Catherine Howard's
custodial set, which is why they appear in the collection despite not using a
Crown address. See [DEMO_SCENARIOS.md](DEMO_SCENARIOS.md) for how to surface them.
